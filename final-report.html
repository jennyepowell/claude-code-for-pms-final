What happened

4.2 changed two things at once, and four responders stopped hearing from us.

On 12 Aug, release 4.2 shortened the ping wait from 90s to 60s and weighted proximity up against acceptance history. Callout tickets jumped from ~6.5 to ~26 a week and stayed there.

A ping nobody answered in time (missed) costs a responder's ranking the same as a "no" (turned down). A lower rank means fewer pings. The score only moves when you are pinged, so a responder who drops can't climb back.

Not proven: the 60s wait clearly caused misses. Whether it, the weight change, or something else collapsed the four responders' rank is not confirmed.

Not in 06-sidekicks/prompts.md: its three prompt slots are empty. This one was typed in the session that built the skill.

The checklist is six questions: customer experience today, the problem, an owner, how we'll know, scope start vs end, and problem before fix. It is saved at .claude/skills/review-checklist/SKILL.md.

Why it matters for 4.2: nobody wrote down how pings are decided, the roadmap went stale, and there is no baseline to say what "worked" looks like. A brief that has to state its problem with evidence, a measure and an owner before it goes forward is harder to ship on opinion.

What it does not do: it reviews briefs. It won't catch a bug in the code, and it can't prove 4.2's own brief lacked these (I haven't seen it).
